# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and reviewers with respect. Discuss linguistic evidence without attacking people. Welcome dialectal variation, document uncertainty and credit sources. Harassment, discrimination and sharing private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to designated maintainers once a confidential contact channel is established. Until then, do not place sensitive reports in public issues. Editors coordinate linguistic disagreements; technical administrators handle operational security. Preserve disagreement records and disclose conflicts of interest.
