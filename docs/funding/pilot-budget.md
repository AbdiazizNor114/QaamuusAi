## Spending order and milestones

1. Confirm account ownership, invoices, source of funds and receipt owner. Reuse existing services where suitable. Establish a private expense ledger before receiving money.
2. Release infrastructure spend only when migration/security/backup checks can make a small contributor pilot usable. See [launch gates](../contributor-launch.md).
3. Approve consent, recording standards, private storage and a two-speaker rehearsal before allocating the remaining collection budget.
4. Pay agreed participation and review costs independently of whether contributors authorize optional TTS/public-release purposes. Confirm terms before work; do not promise payment without funds.
5. Approve evaluation compute only with a reproducible plan and a hard operator-controlled stop budget. No always-on GPU, training run or paid API without a revised estimate and approval.
6. Report monthly totals: opening funds, receipts, expenses by category, committed payments, remaining funds, accepted minutes and unresolved gates. Publish aggregates, never participant identities or billing secrets. Sponsor recognition does not buy editorial decisions.
