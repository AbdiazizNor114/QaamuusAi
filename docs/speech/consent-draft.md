# Participant information and consent — working draft

**Not ready for collection or signatures.** Fill the organization, contact, dates and retention details; obtain qualified privacy review and an independently reviewed Somali version before use. This document proposes a process and does not determine the project's lawful basis or provide final legal terms.

## Information to explain before recording

- Responsible organization and data steward: **not appointed**. Participant contact and complaints route: **must be confirmed**.
- Purpose: develop and evaluate Somali language and speech tools. Voice, transcripts and participant contact information may be personal data. Pseudonyms do not make voice anonymous.
- What is collected: chosen recordings, transcripts, optional self-described dialect, device/recording metadata and evidence of permission. Explain who can access each item, storage region, processors, transfers and security arrangements.
- Separate purposes: speech recognition, evaluation, synthetic speech/voice development, and public distribution of audio/transcripts. Describe whether model weights or datasets may later be openly distributed and whether commercial reuse is possible under the chosen licence.
- Participation is voluntary. Explain payment before participation; declining optional purposes must not be disguised as agreement to everything. Do not promise an opportunity or service that does not exist.
- State actual retention periods, participant rights, how to raise a request and the applicable limits. Explain before agreement that public copies may persist and trained models may not be fully reversible; do not promise removal from every downstream copy or model.

## Separate choices to finalize

Use unticked choices and retain the exact information version and timestamp privately. Participation/recording is a separate decision from each downstream purpose:

- [ ] I choose to participate after reading or hearing the information and having my questions answered.
- [ ] I allow the described speech-recognition development use.
- [ ] I allow the described evaluation use.
- [ ] I separately allow the described synthetic speech / voice development use.
- [ ] I separately allow the described public release of recordings and transcripts under the specifically explained licence.

These checkboxes are a design draft, not a claim that a single checkbox settles all data-protection or intellectual-property requirements. Resolve licence/purpose contradictions before collection. ASR-only or evaluation-only permission never silently becomes TTS permission. Do not publicly release under an open licence while promising research-only downstream use.

## Private permission record

Record consent ID, random speaker ID, information/translation version, each choice, timestamp, operator, agreed compensation and any subsequent change/withdrawal. Keep participant identity and contact separately; access is restricted. Do not publish signatures or email addresses in dataset manifests.

## Request handling proposal

A steward acknowledges requests within five working days as an internal target, verifies identity proportionately and applies the governing legal deadlines after review. Immediately pause new uses of disputed clips, update permission status, remove them from future exports/jobs, address controlled copies and notify processors/recipients as appropriate. Document decisions, remaining copies, backup expiry and any justified exceptions. Public-release limitations must be explained honestly; technical difficulty does not itself settle legal obligations.

Reference for reviewer: [European Commission — individual data-protection rights](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en). Determine the applicable rules for the actual organization and participants before launch.
